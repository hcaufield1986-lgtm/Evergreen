The Evergreen Standard

Quality isn't a slogan. It's a process.

Anyone can roll paint on a wall. The finish only lasts — and only looks right — when the steps before it are done properly. Here's ours:

  1. Walk & quote. We look at the whole job, measure, and give you an honest written estimate. Free, no pressure.
  2. Protect. Floors, furniture, and fixtures get covered and masked before a can opens.
  3. Prep. Fill, sand, caulk, scrape, and prime. This is the step most painters rush — we never do.
  4. Paint. Premium paints, proper coverage, crisp lines at every edge and corner.
  5. Walkthrough. We inspect the finished work together and don't leave until you're happy.
